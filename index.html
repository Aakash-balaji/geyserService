Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our geyser repair, maintenance, and doorstep services in Hyderabad & Secunderabad.

What types and brands of geysers do you repair in Hyderabad?

We repair instant and storage electric geysers from major brands including AO Smith, Bajaj, Havells, Racold, Venus, Crompton, V-Guard, Kenstar, and Orient. Share your model and issue when booking so we can check compatible spare parts.

How quickly can a geyser technician arrive at my doorstep?

We offer same-day doorstep service across Hyderabad and Secunderabad, with a target arrival within 90 minutes. Call or WhatsApp us to confirm technician availability and the arrival time for your area.

Why is my geyser not heating water?

A faulty heating element, thermostat, thermal cut-out, or electrical connection can cause heating problems. Our technician inspects the water heater and explains the repair and price before starting work.

When should a geyser be descaled?

Hard water can leave mineral deposits inside the tank and on the heating element. If heating becomes slower, the geyser makes unusual noises, or hot-water flow drops, book an inspection. Descaling frequency depends on water hardness and usage.

What is included in geyser servicing?

Servicing includes inspection of the heating element, thermostat, anode rod, inlet and outlet connections, pressure safety valve, and electrical connections. Tank flushing, descaling, or replacement parts are recommended after inspection and quoted before work.

Do you provide a warranty on geyser repair and parts?

We offer a 1-year service warranty. Ask the technician to confirm the coverage, eligible replacement parts, and any exclusions for your repair before approving the work.
